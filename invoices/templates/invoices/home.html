Four ways it shapes sound

Each part of the engine works on its own, but they're built to talk to each other while you play.

Adaptive engine

Tracks velocity, pressure, and timing, then adjusts timbre continuously instead of snapping between fixed states.

Spatial field

Places every voice in a three-dimensional field so layered patches stay distinct instead of collapsing into mud.

Live morphing

Draw a path between two sounds and Aurelia performs the transition in real time, with no audible seams.

Preset intelligence

Learns from the patches you keep and starts suggesting variations tuned to your own sound, not a generic library.

Built around a single oscillator core

Most engines stack effects on top of a static waveform. Aurelia does it in reverse — the oscillator itself deforms in response to what you play, so the character of the sound and the way you shape it come from the same source.

  • One core, no bolted-on effects chain to fight with
  • Full MPE support across every controller we've tested
  • Runs natively on CPU — no dedicated GPU required
